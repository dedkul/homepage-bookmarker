Lumen v0.5

Three styles, each with two variations. On any page, open Customize (bottom-right) to switch style and variation, rows, background, overlay and theme. Press / to search.

Style 01Dock

I · Still: centred and calm, with a magnifying dock.
II · Horizon: time sits low, and the dock hides until your cursor nears it.

 Style 02Editorial

I · Broadsheet: a serif masthead with a typographic index of favorites.
II · Gallery: the background is the artwork, with labels at the edges.

 Style 03Bento

I · Soft: rounded glass tiles with tasks and notes.
II · Mono: an instrument panel with a hairline grid and monospaced type.
